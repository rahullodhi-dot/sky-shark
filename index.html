Meet Sky Shark · Where Pilots Are Made

We build pilots who understand the sky, read the mountain and land safely. Every single time.

Sky Shark Aero School trains from the slopes of Bir Billing, one of the best-known flying sites in the world. Whether it is your first day on a hill or your hundredth cross-country, every session starts with a clear plan and a clear head. Taught in English, हिन्दी and Hinglish.

Safety & training approach

Safe pilots
fly longer.

Our rule is simple. If the air is not right, we do not fly. These are the habits every student learns from day one.

01
Before anyone leaves the ground

Read the weather

Wind, thermals and cloud are checked before anyone leaves the ground. Flights are cancelled when conditions are not good.

WindSteady, not gusty
ThermalsGentle, not too strong
CloudClear of the ridge
No-goGo
Your instructor makes the call every morning, and says no when conditions are not good.
02
Before every flight

Check every piece of gear

Wing, lines, harness, helmet and reserve are inspected before each flight, and students learn to do it themselves.

  • Wing and cells
  • Lines and risers
  • Harness and buckles
  • Helmet and radio
  • Reserve parachute
You learn the check yourself, so it becomes a habit for life.
03
Plan the flight

Brief, then fly

A clear plan for launch, route and landing, with radio contact for students throughout.

LaunchWind check, lines clear, radio call
RouteWhere to fly, turn and stay clear
LandingApproach agreed before take-off
Your instructor talks you through every flight, start to finish.
04
Build skill in order

Ground before sky

Wing handling on the slope comes first. You progress when you are ready, not on a fixed schedule.

01Ground handling
02Slope hops
03Low flights
04High flights
05Solo
You move up a step when you are ready, not on a fixed schedule.
